Membership Tiers & Benefits

Non-Profit (NP) Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: R0 (Free for verified NPOs)

  • Standard listing in the Joburg Vacation Chambers online directory
  • Invitations to quarterly community tourism & heritage mixers
  • Access to monthly Gauteng travel trend newsletters
  • Ability to post community events on the chamber calendar

Bronze Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: R1,500 / year

  • All Non-Profit Membership benefits included
  • Enhanced directory card with logo, website link, and booking contact
  • 10% discount on Joburg Tourism Expo tickets and workshops
  • Digital "Proud Member" badges for your website and social channels

Silver Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: R3,500 / year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits included
  • Eligible for randomized Homepage Spotlight advertisements
  • 25% discount on annual tourism workshops and hospitality training
  • Inclusion in seasonal travel guides (Family, Couples, and Safari itineraries)
  • Two complimentary passes to quarterly networking luncheons

Gold Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: R6,500 / year

  • All Silver Membership benefits included
  • Priority Homepage Spotlight placement & featured package promotion
  • Complimentary exhibition booth at the annual Joburg Tourism Expo
  • 50% discount on all staff hospitality & digital marketing training
  • Direct warm leads from the "Plan My Trip" custom itinerary builder
