Website Project Plan: Joburg Vacation Chambers

Planning Document • Plan Less. Explore More. Love Johannesburg.

1. Site Name

Joburg Vacation Chambers (Suggested domain: www.joburgvacationchambers.co.za)

Reason Selected: The name clearly connects vacation planning and local tourism coordination with Johannesburg and Gauteng, giving locals, first-time international visitors, and tourism businesses a memorable, trustworthy identity.

2. Site Purpose

Joburg Vacation Chambers serves as a planning and discovery hub for trips in and around Johannesburg, including Soweto, Pretoria, Magaliesburg, and Pilanesberg. The site provides:

  • Curated vacation packages, day trips, and weekend getaways for families, couples, adventure seekers, and budget travelers.
  • Destination guides, travel tips, upcoming local events, and live Johannesburg weather information.
  • An interactive trip planning flow, member directory and spotlights, and a membership application form for local operators.

3. Visitor Scenarios

  • Where can I find a safe, kid-friendly weekend getaway near Johannesburg with clear pricing and activities for the whole family?
  • How can a first-time visitor plan a multi-day itinerary covering Johannesburg, Soweto, and a nearby safari with guided transport?
  • What romantic getaway options or sunset experiences are available near Johannesburg, and how do I contact the team to book?
  • How can a local tour, lodge, or hospitality business join the chamber or view featured member listings?

4. Color Scheme

This document uses the planned vacation palette exclusively:

  • Primary Color — #0E6B7A (Teal Blue): Used for the page header, footer, section borders, and primary headings to convey trust and travel energy.
  • Secondary / Accent Color — #F2A541 (Sunset Orange): Used for header/footer accent dividers, callout borders, and visual highlights.
  • Background Color — #FFFBF5 (Warm Off-White): Used as the main page background and inside callout/swatch panels for a welcoming feel.
  • Text Color — #222222 (Charcoal): Used for body copy, subheadings, and readable contrast across light surfaces.

5. Typography

Two Google Fonts are selected for the site and applied across this planning document:

  • Poppins: Used for headings (h1, h2, h3) to give section titles a friendly, modern vacation feel.
  • Inter: Used for body text, lists, and descriptions for clean legibility on both mobile and desktop screens.
